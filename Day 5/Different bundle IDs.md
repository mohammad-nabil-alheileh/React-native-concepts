# Different bundle IDs

A bundle ID is the unique name that identifies your app to the phone and to the store. On Android it is called the `applicationId`, and on iOS it is the bundle identifier.

Two apps with the same ID are the same app to the phone. Installing one over the other replaces it. Different IDs make them different apps that sit side by side.

## Rules for choosing an ID

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Lowercase, such as `com.company.app` |
| Characters | Letters, numbers, and underscores only |
| Permanence | Choose it once, carefully. After publishing it is permanent |
